## Identification evidence

### Introductory Mantras

The opening contains invocations including `Nityānandāya gurave`, `Om saha nāvavatu` and the closing `Om śāntiḥ śāntiḥ śāntiḥ`. The next section begins with the first Guru Pādukā verse at approximately 02:26.65. The boundary sits inside a short quiet gap immediately before the opening `Om`.

### Śrī Guru Pādukā Pañcakam

The transcription recognises the recurring `śrī-guru-pādukābhyām` ending and the final `Om śāntiḥ śāntiḥ śāntiḥ`. The transition into the Guru Gītā occurs inside a clear quiet interval around 05:16.8–05:18.7.

### Śrī Guru Gītā

The opening invocation begins immediately after the quiet interval. Near 48:09–48:17 the recitation explicitly identifies the conclusion of the Guru Gītā. A short quiet transition follows around 48:18.8–48:19.5.

### Śrī Avadhūta Stotram

The next chant begins `Nityānandāya gurave śiṣya-saṃsāra-hāriṇe`. This identifies it as Śrī Avadhūta Stotram, rather than the similarly named `Nityānandaṃ parama-sukhadaṃ` verse. It concludes with peace and welfare verses and `Om śāntiḥ` around 54:09.

### Jyota se Jyota Jagao

The Hindi bhajan begins around 54:12 with the recurring `Sadguru, jyota se jyota jagao` refrain. The boundary was moved to 54:11.6 after listening showed that the earlier cut included the final second of the preceding chant. The same final track contains closing Sanskrit verses, including `Pūrṇamadaḥ pūrṇamidam` and `Om śāntiḥ śāntiḥ śāntiḥ`, followed by a final salutation.
